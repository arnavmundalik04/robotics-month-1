Topics covered: Electron flow, current flow, voltage and multimeter basics as well. 
Voltage: It’s the reason current flows. It is the difference in electrical potential energy, per unit charge, between two points. 
LED: Much more efficient than filament bulbs. They usually need some standard forward voltage before they start emitting light (Vf). 
Voltage x Current = Power 
Power in a resistor: V x I = V x (V/R) = V2/R. Hence if there is a lot of voltage going through a resistor, it can heat up due to excess power consumption.
