Day 8
We’re learning about multimeters today. 
Plugs going into the multimeter are called banana plugs
10A is the port to measure high currents, 10A is the highest this multimeter can take.
COM means the common and is usually connected to the negative end of the circuit.
mAV can measure small current, voltage and resistance. 
I tried out measuring resistors, I tried out measuring battery voltage, a diode, different values of the resistor I even made a full circuit with a resistor & LED. I also learned more about the continuity of a breadboard using a multimeter. The main goal with building and messing up a circuit is to get into the rhythm of “How to find out what went wrong by measuring the continuity or the flow of current/voltage rather than randomly switching wires up. Ask these questions. 
•	Is the battery providing voltage?
•	Is there continuity through suspected wires?
•	Is voltage reaching the component?
•	Where does the expected electrical path stop?
Think:
“The LED isn't working. What can I measure to determine why?”
This is the foundation of debugging robotics.
Power → connection → voltage → component → signal → mechanical system.
Use measurements to eliminate possibilities instead of guessing.
